Fictional Networking Vendor: NetCore
Application Name: TAC Desk

TAC Desk handles 2000+ escalated support tickets every year across 3 different regions
AMER
EMEA
APJC

Every ticket follows the lifecycle
OPEN -> TRIAGE -> DIAGNOSE -> FIX -> VERIFY -> CLOSE -> RCA

The problem
Leadership keeps hearing the same complaints
Customers - Your’ ETAs are meaningless, you said four hours - but it look 2 days
Account Managers - I find about SLA breaches after the customer has already escalated to our VP
Duty Managers - I can’t tell which tickets in the queue will turn into LONG RUNNING ONE, so I can’t staff the shifts properly

Today, ETA come from a fixed value | rule
S1 - 4 Hours
S2 - 1 Day
S3 - 2 Days
S4 - 3 Days

The business question
When a ticket is triaged, can we predict how many hours it will take to resolve?

Business Metrics already leadership team tracks
MTTR (Mean time to resolve) - Average Resolution Hours
SLA Breach Rate
Tickets Volume | Backlog

Common Data Structure
Ticket ID (TKT-000001, TKT-000002)
Created At (timestamp across 2025 - mostly on weekdays between 0800 AM to 0800 PM)
Product Family (Routers, Switches, Firewalls, Wireless, SD-WAN) - Switches and Routes are common
Severity (S1, S2, S3 and S4 with proababilities 5%, 20%, 45% and 30%)
Customer Tier (Platinum, Gold, Silver)
Region (AMER, EMEA, APJC)
Channel (Email, Phone, Portal)
Devices Affected (integer at least 1, right skewed (most 1-3, and a few about 50))
Software Version in Months (integer 0-60)
Prior Ticket in 90 Days (integer 0 to about 15)
Engineer Experience in Years (float - 0.5 - 20 - one decimal should be fine)
First Response in Minutes (float, faster for S1/S2 and Platinum customers)
Customer Comments (one about 10 realistic comments including phrases about product family)
Escalation Count (integer 0-5)
Reopened (boolean, about 8% true, more likely for longer tickets)
Resolution Code (Config Fix, Software Bug, Hardware RMA, Customer Environment - Hardware RMA and Software Bug tickets take much longer)
SLA Breached Or Not (resolution hours above the SLA for the severity - s2 should have the highest breach rate)
Resolution in Hours (positive integer - one decimal)
